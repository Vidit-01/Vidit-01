Human Factor & Hobbies / 2024

Beyond the Binary:
The Human Factor &
Intellectual Curiosities.

Computational rigor often obscures the irrational, beautiful mess that is human curiosity. While my primary research focuses on Machine Learning and Data Science, the true catalyst for my work lies in the "Human Factor"—the qualitative bridge between digital logic and subjective experience. This section serves as an archive of the non-linear influences that shape my professional output.

[07.1]

The Human Factor

Interaction is not just about throughput or efficiency; it is about the semiotics of the interface. In my exploration of human-digital interaction, I examine how we map cognitive models onto rigid silicon structures.

It is the study of why we feel a certain way when a transition is smooth, or how a specific visualization can trigger a moment of 'Aha!' that raw data never could. My interest lies in quantifying these qualitative shifts using modern ML techniques.
